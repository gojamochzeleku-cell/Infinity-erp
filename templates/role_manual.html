The Master Filing Blueprint

Company Isolation: The archive room is split. Left side is CHI (Capital), Right side is YAH (Yirga). Never mix them.

🗄️ The 4 Master Cabinets (Per Company)

  • CAB-01: Tenders & Ops (Blue Binders)
  • CAB-02: Finance (Black Binders)
  • CAB-03: Legal & HR (Red / Green Binders)
  • CAB-04: Mail & Admin (Grey Binders)

📚 Shelf Partitions

  • TOP Partition: Active, Current, and Ongoing files.
  • BOTTOM Partition: Closed, Archived, or Lost files.

🏷️ How to Label the Binder Spine

  • Line 1: Company (CHI or YAH)
  • Line 2: Binder Name (e.g., ACTIVE TENDERS)
  • Line 3: Year (e.g., 2026)
The Daily A-B-C Procedure

🅰️ Step 1: Prepare the Paper

Upload the file to the ERP to get a Doc ID. Print it. Stick the printed QR Code on the top right corner. Hole-punch the paper.

🅱️ Step 2: File the Paper

Go to the correct cabinet and shelf partition. Pull the correct binder. Open it and find the tab (Tender ID, Month, or Employee Name). Clip the paper behind the tab.

🅲 Step 3: Update the ERP

Click Record Physical Filing in the ERP. Use the drop-downs to select the exact Cabinet, Shelf, and Binder you used. Type the Tab name.

Check-out & Tracking
  • Check-out: If a manager takes a physical file, click Check-out to Employee. When returned, click Mark as Returned.
  • Scanning: Use 🔍 Scan Document on the dashboard. Type the Doc ID to instantly find where the paper is located.
  • Expiries: When filing a document, set an Expiry Date if applicable (e.g., Work Permits). It will alert the CEO 30 days before expiry.
