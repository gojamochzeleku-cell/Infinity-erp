📖 Office Assistant Training Manual
Infinity Investment Institute ERP • Version 1.0

Welcome to the Infinity ERP. As the Office Assistant, you are the Digital and Physical Bridge of the company. This manual will guide you through your daily tasks. Tap on any section to read it.

1. The Tender Chain (Your Tasks)

You have several critical tasks in the Tender chain. You will be notified in your Action Center when it's your turn.

T08: Upload Purchased Bid Document

After the Case Executive buys the physical bid document, they upload the receipt. The task then moves to you. You must scan the purchased bid document and upload it.

Important: The moment you upload this document, the AI engine will read it and automatically generate 4 new tasks for the Bid Manager, Finance, HR, and Bid Operator.

T14: Upload Bank Letter

Finance will prepare a bank letter for the bid security. You must upload this letter, which automatically registers it in the "Outgoing Mail" registry.

T20, T21, T24: Uploads

If the company wins, you will be tasked to upload the Award Document, Draft Agreement, and Final Signed Agreement. Always ensure these are scanned clearly.

2. Document Registry & Standard Filing

Every file uploaded in the system gets a unique Document ID (e.g., CHI-DOC-0001). You are responsible for the physical replica.

How to file a document:

  • Go to Docs in the menu or dashboard.
  • Find a document marked "Not Physically Filed".
  • Click Record Physical Filing.
  • Fill in the hierarchy: Room ➔ Cabinet ➔ Shelf ➔ Box ➔ Folder.

How to Check-Out a Document:

If a manager needs a physical document, click Check-out to Employee. The system will mark it as "Currently with [Name]". When they return it, click Mark as Returned.

Pro Tip: Use the 🔍 Scan Document button on your dashboard. If you type a Doc ID, it will instantly pull up the file and tell you exactly where the physical paper is stored.
3. Mail Registry (Incoming & Outgoing)

You manage all formal letters that enter or leave the company.

Incoming Mail:

When a letter arrives (e.g., from the Ministry), go to Mail ➔ + Incoming. Upload the scan, enter the Sender, and assign it to the CEO or relevant manager. This creates a task in their Action Center to read it.

Outgoing Mail:

When the company sends a letter, go to Mail ➔ + Outgoing. Important: Set a "Reply Deadline". If the recipient doesn't reply by that date, the system will alert management that the letter is overdue.

4. The Office Command Center

Your dedicated dashboard widget for physical office management.

🚚 Log Dispatch (Courier)

When you send a document via a driver or courier (e.g., submitting a bid to the Ministry), log it here. Enter the driver's name and the destination.

👔 Visitor Check-in

When guests arrive, log their name and who they are visiting. This helps security and allows the host to know their guest is waiting in the lobby.

5. Supplies & General Uploads

Stationery Requests

Employees will request pens, paper, or toner through their dashboard. You will receive a task in your Action Center. Once you give them the items, go to the Supply Requests page and click Mark as Fulfilled.

General Uploads

If you need to upload a document that isn't part of a Tender (e.g., an ISO certificate or Trade License), use the ⬆️ Upload Document button on your Command Center. Don't forget to select the correct category so it gets filed correctly.

6. IT Support

If your computer breaks, the printer jams, or you cannot log in to the system, use the 🎫 Submit IT Ticket button on your dashboard. The IT Coordinator will be notified immediately and will come to assist you.
